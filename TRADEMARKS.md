# Trademarks

"x3ro deck" and the x3ro deck logo (`data/icons/x3ro-deck.svg` and the images
made from it) identify the official project and its releases.

The code is free software under the GPL (see `LICENSE`), and you may fork,
modify and redistribute it under those terms. Section 7(e) of the license
adds that it grants no rights to the name or the logo. So:

- **You may** say that your project is based on x3ro deck, link to it, and
  describe it accurately.
- **You may not** call a modified version "x3ro deck", or use the logo for
  it, or suggest that it is the official project or endorsed by it. Give it
  a name and an icon of its own.
- **Unmodified builds** of an official release may be redistributed under
  the name, for example in a distribution's package repository.

The credit "x3ro deck by x3ro" in the About dialog must stay in modified
versions; that is the attribution term in section 7(b) of `LICENSE` and is
separate from this policy.

For anything this doesn't cover, ask the maintainers before using the name
or logo.
